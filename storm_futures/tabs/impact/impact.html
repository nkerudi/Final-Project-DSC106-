Behind Every Storm: The Human Cost of Our Climate Choices

The projections above describe a physical future like warmer oceans and more intense storms. But what do they mean for people? Since 1980, tropical cyclones have caused more economic damage than any other U.S. weather disaster. The emissions path we choose today sets the scale of that toll for the rest of this century.

Costs are accelerating. The five most expensive tropical cyclone years on record have all occurred since 2004. As we've seen through earlier visualizations, ocean temperatures are warming causing an extended hurricane season and intensified peak storms. The damage curve steepens, making the emissions choices explored in this project directly legible in dollar terms and in lives.
