What do these scenarios mean?

Each line is a Shared Socioeconomic Pathway (SSP) — a scenario of future human choices agreed upon by an international group of climate scientists. The number after the dash indicates how much extra heat the atmosphere traps by 2100, measured in watts per square meter. Higher = more warming.

Data sources

  • CMIP6 — Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6
    Climate model simulations produced by NOAA's Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory (GFDL-ESM4). GHG concentration data accessed via the NOAA Public CMIP6 Dataset on Google Cloud. Variables used: co2s (surface CO₂, Emon table), ch4 and n2o (Amon table).
  • SSP scenario definitions
    Shared Socioeconomic Pathways defined by the World Climate Research Programme (WCRP) and described in the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (AR6), Working Group I.
  • Processing
    Monthly global grids collapsed to annual global means using cosine-latitude weighted spatial averaging. Units converted from mole fractions (mol mol⁻¹) to ppm (CO₂) and ppb (CH₄, N₂O). Radiative forcing derived using the standard IPCC log formula: RF = 5.35 × ln(C / 278).
