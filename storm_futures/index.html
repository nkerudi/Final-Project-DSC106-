So far we have solidified the purpose behind our project. We wish to showcase how different future climate metrics are impacted under different emission scenarios, with our chosen topics being greenhouse gases, sea surface temperatures, and land surface temperatures. We have gathered and processed the required data to create our visuals as well as created initial prototypes with Python. Our next task is to load and render them using D3.

The most challenging part of our project to design will most likely be making a cohesive and easy to digest story. Since we are working with many different climate, social, and scientific metrics, finding a way to properly explain everything and tie it all into a narrative that non-experts can understand is our biggest challenge.
