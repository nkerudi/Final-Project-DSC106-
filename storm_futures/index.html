How do human climate choices reshape the storms of our future?

Explore how tropical storms and hurricanes have evolved in the past to the present, and how they are projected to evolve in the future under different greenhouse gas emissions scenarios. This story uses a variety of data from the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6) ensemble of climate models, which are used by the IPCC to understand past, present, and future climate conditions.
